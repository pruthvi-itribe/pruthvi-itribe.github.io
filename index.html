Things I've built

A white-label trading terminal for Indian brokers

We put fifteen different broker APIs behind a single interface, so a new broker could sign and go live in the same quarter. The terminal ran inside a large broker's app for about 75K traders a day, and four engineers looked after web, mobile and every partner deployment, mostly because I rebuilt how we worked around AI coding agents.

  • 15 broker APIs
  • 75K+ daily traders
  • 99.9% uptime
  • 4 engineers

iTribe, a social investing app

It peaked at 100K monthly users and 250K downloads, and the backend served more than 10M requests a month at under 100ms P99. When the bill started to matter we moved the Kubernetes and Terraform estate from AWS to DigitalOcean and cut cloud spend by about 90%. We later pivoted the company from B2C to B2B on unit economics and got it operationally cash-positive.

  • 100K MAU
  • 10M+ req/month
  • <100ms P99
  • ~90% cloud cut

Walmart's customer identity graph

It links the many records of one shopper into a single identity, across 115B nodes and 75B edges on Apache Spark, and personalization, attribution and lifetime value all run on top of it. I brought graph computation down from weeks to hours, and the patent for the approach is in my name.

  • 115B nodes
  • weeks → hours
  • US Patent 11,556,370
